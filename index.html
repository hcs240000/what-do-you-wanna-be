I can’t remember the exact dates, but when I was growing up I wanted to be so many things. I wanted to write books. I wanted to work in a library. I wanted to be a fashion designer and draw new and exciting clothing. At one point I wanted to be a chef and got really into watching the Food Network. I loved baking so maybe I figured I could be a pastry chef.

When you’re little, everything seems possible because the world is huge and you have your whole life ahead of you.
